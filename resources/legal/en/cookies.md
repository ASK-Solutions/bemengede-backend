# Cookie Policy

**Effective:** {{effective}}

Cookies and similar browser storage (local and session storage) are small pieces of data that websites save on your device. We use storage rather than cookies, but the same rules apply. This policy covers the {{trade_name}} website. The mobile app does not use cookies. It stores only your sign-in token, in your phone's secure storage.

## Strictly necessary (always on)

These are needed for the site to work. You cannot switch them off, but they contain no tracking.

| Name | Where it's stored | Purpose | Lasts |
|---|---|---|---|
| `bm_consent` | Local storage | Remembers your cookie choice, its version and a random ID (not linked to you) | Until you change it, or until this policy changes |
| `bm_session` | Session storage | Keeps you signed in while the tab is open | Until you close the tab |
| `bm_booking`, `bm_phone_*` | Session storage | Remembers the booking you are paying for | Until you close the tab |

## Optional analytics (off unless you allow them)

If you choose **Allow analytics**, we would load privacy-friendly, aggregated analytics to understand which pages work and which confuse people. **No analytics tool is installed at launch.** If we add one, we will list its name, provider and cookies here before turning it on, and it will load only after you say yes.

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Our fonts are served from our own website, so loading a page does not send your IP address to a font provider.

## Your choice

When you first visit, a banner gives you two equal options: **Reject optional** and **Allow analytics**. You can change your mind at any time with the **Cookie settings** link in the footer. We ask again only if this policy changes.
