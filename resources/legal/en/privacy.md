**Effective:** {{effective}}

This policy explains what personal data {{trade_name}} collects when you search for, book or manage a bus trip on our website or app, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It is written to meet Ethiopia's Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024.

For a booking, we and the bus company that operates your trip each act as a controller of your data. The bus company uses it to run the trip, board passengers and handle complaints.

## What we collect, and why

We collect only what we need to sell and check your ticket.

| Data | Why we need it | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile phone number | Send your ticket and one-time sign-in codes, contact you about changes to your trip | Contract |
| Passenger full name(s) | Printed on the boarding manifest, which the bus company must keep | Contract, legal obligation |
| Seat number and fare type (adult, child, student) | Issue the right ticket at the right price | Contract |
| Email address (optional) | Send a copy of your ticket by email | Contract |
| Payment records (method, amount, transaction reference, status) | Confirm payment, issue refunds, prevent fraud, keep accounting records | Contract, legal obligation |
| Bank transfer reference you enter | Check your transfer with verify.et | Contract |
| Marketing choices | Send offers only if you said yes | Consent |
| Cookie choice | Remember whether you allowed optional cookies | Legal obligation |
| Basic technical logs (IP address, device type, errors) | Keep the service secure and working | Legitimate interest |

**What we do not collect:** birth date, gender, ID or passport number, location tracking, contacts, photos, or your card or wallet number. Card and wallet details are entered on the payment provider's own page, never on ours.

## Children

You must be 18 or older to create an account or make a booking. Children can travel as passengers on a booking made by a parent, guardian or other responsible adult, who provides the child's name. We do not knowingly create accounts for anyone under 18. If you believe a child has created an account, contact {{privacy_email}} and we will delete it.

## Who we share data with

We never sell your personal data. We share it only as needed with:

- **The bus company** operating your trip: passenger names, seat numbers, the contact phone and booking status.
- **Payment providers**: Chapa, telebirr (Ethio Telecom), or verify.et for bank transfer checks. They receive the amount, a payment reference and, for Chapa and telebirr, the phone number or email needed to complete the payment.
- **SMS and email providers** that deliver your ticket and sign-in codes.
- **Hosting and infrastructure providers** that store our data under contract.
- **Authorities**, when the law requires it.

A full list of the third-party services we use is available on request from {{privacy_email}}.

## International transfers

Some providers may process data outside Ethiopia. We transfer data abroad only where the Ethiopian Communications Authority has found adequate protection, or with appropriate safeguards such as approved contract clauses, as the Proclamation requires.

## How long we keep it

- Bookings and tickets: while your account is open, and for up to 2 years after your last trip so you can see your history and raise complaints.
- Payment and accounting records: {{retention_years}} years, as required by tax and accounting law. When you delete your account, we remove your name and contact details from these records and keep only amounts, dates and references.
- One-time sign-in codes: 5 minutes.
- Marketing consent records: for as long as you are subscribed, plus proof that you unsubscribed.

## Your rights

You can:

- **Access** your data: download a copy from *Account › Privacy › Download my data*.
- **Correct** it: edit your name and email in your account, or contact us.
- **Delete** it: *Account › Privacy › Delete my account*. Deletion happens after a {{cooling_off_hours}}-hour period during which you can change your mind. If you have an upcoming paid trip, please cancel it or wait until after you travel.
- **Withdraw consent** to marketing at any time, in one click, from any message or in your account. This does not affect your tickets.
- **Object** to processing based on legitimate interest.
- **Complain** to the Ethiopian Communications Authority if you think we have handled your data unlawfully.

To use any right not available in the app, email {{privacy_email}}. We reply within 30 days.

## Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed sign-in codes, access controls that limit each bus company to its own passengers, and audit logs for every change to a booking or payment. If a data breach puts you at high risk, we will tell you without undue delay and notify the Ethiopian Communications Authority within 72 hours.

## Changes

If we change this policy in a way that affects you, we will show the new version in the app and on the website before it applies. Where the law requires, we will ask for your agreement again.
