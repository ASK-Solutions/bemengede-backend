# Refund Policy

**Effective:** {{effective}}

This policy explains when you get money back and how. The exact cancellation window for your trip is set by the bus company and is shown before you pay and on your ticket.

## You cancel

| When you cancel | What you get back |
|---|---|
| Before the Operator's free-cancellation deadline (usually 48 hours before departure) | 100% of the fare |
| After the deadline, before departure | The fare minus the Operator's late-cancellation fee (usually 20%) |
| After the bus has departed, or if you miss the bus | No refund |

The service fee (ETB {{service_fee}} per seat) covers the booking service already provided, so it is not refunded when you cancel. It **is** refunded if the Operator cancels.

You can cancel in the app or on the website under *My trips*. Before you confirm, we show the exact amount you will get back.

## The Operator cancels or delays

If the Operator cancels your trip, or delays it by more than 3 hours, you get a **full refund including the service fee**, or a free move to another trip by the same Operator.

## Payment problems

- If you were charged but did not receive a ticket, contact us. We check with the payment provider and either issue the ticket or refund you in full.
- If you were charged twice, we refund the duplicate in full.

## How and when you get your money

- Refunds go back to the method you paid with: telebirr, Chapa or the bank account the transfer came from.
- We start the refund within 2 working days. The provider usually completes it within 7 working days.
- Cash bought at a terminal counter is refunded at that counter.
